From agency concept to shippable app

An agency delivered a bold new booking flow. I turned it into a product-ready system for European travellers, safe to ship and test.

01

Problem

The agency concept looked fresh, but it was not built for the real product, its data or daily business.

Product readyEvery screen fits the real product, its data and its components.
Proven by dataEach change ships behind an A/B test as a safeguard.
Safe for businessNo negative impact on daily bookings during rollout.
02

Approach

I worked through the funnel one step at a time, and gave each step one clear principle.

For every step I put the current app, the agency draft and my own versions side by side, then kept only what fit the product and the people using it.

03

Shared components

Four core components carry the same logic from listing to payment, so the whole flow feels like one product.

Price breakdownPrice and flight split into two clear parts.
Flight summaryLight modules that expand when needed on mobile.
Flight cardSimplified to highlight the key information.
Booking summaryOne final summary that brings every detail together.

Where the agency and I chose differently, data and the real product decided.

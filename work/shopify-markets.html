SHOPIFY · GOOGLE APP · 2022

One store, many countries on Google

Shopify merchants sell across borders, but the Google app only let them advertise in one country. I redesigned targeting so one store can reach every market it ships to.

Lead product designer · Shopify Google app · 2022

#2merchant frustration in the Google app

01

Problem

A quick fix that stayed.

Free listings, Smart Shopping and Buy on Google could target only one country. It started as a short-term shortcut and was never revisited, until it became merchants’ second biggest frustration with the app.

What merchants asked for

“Get more sales in different countries”
“Target the EU as a whole with our international domain settings”
“Target multiple German markets at the same time, and we don’t want to use a paid app”
02

The rules I designed around

Google decides where ads can run, so the design had to make its limits clear.

Language and shippingWhere ads appear depends on the store’s language and shipping zones.
Supported countriesNot every country is supported by Google.
Buy on GoogleOnly available in the United States.
03

Key decisions

Each decision made multiple markets feel like one simple setting.

1 · Settings

Use the markets merchants already set up

Countries come straight from Shopify Markets and are grouped the same way. Merchants pick from what they already sell to, instead of building a second list.

3 · Product status

Show status per country

A product can be approved in some countries and rejected in others. A single yes or no would hide that, so each variant shows exactly where it is not approved.

More Google channel work

YouTube ShoppingLed design across Shopify and YouTube so merchants can sell on YouTube through the Google app. Google can’t explain why it rejects a store, so I spelled out every eligibility rule up front.

06

What I learned

A shortcut in settings becomes a ceiling for growth.

Working within another company’s rules taught me to design the explanation as carefully as the feature.

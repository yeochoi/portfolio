SHOPIFY · 2022 TO 2023

Getting merchants paid at the right moment

Merchants had to choose between charging buyers too early or capturing every payment by hand. I designed a third option, automatic capture when the order ships.

Sole product designer · 1 PM and 10 engineers · Shopify admin

01

Problem

Shopify made merchants ship an order before they could take the money, and that broke real businesses.

It started with orders from Facebook checkout, where 2% to 8% of merchants were stuck. A shop making personalised products needs payment before it makes anything, and some fulfilment apps could not handle an order that was not yet paid.

What 6 merchant interviews showed

Hidden deadlinesMerchants could not see when a payment would expire.
Long lead timesPayment windows were shorter than the time needed to make or restock.
Split ordersCapturing payment for partly shipped orders was hard.
Different products, different needsOne capture rule did not fit every product type.
02

Two steps

A quick fix for Facebook orders first, then one setting for every merchant.

Step 1 · Interim fixFacebook orders now follow the merchant's own capture setting, the same as online store orders. One mental model instead of two.
Step 2 · Long-term solutionA new capture mode for all Shopify merchants, automatic capture when the order is fulfilled. It moved to build in spring 2023.
03

Key decisions

Each decision balanced clarity for merchants with what engineering could ship.

1 · Settings

A third capture mode

Merchants can now capture at checkout, when the order is fulfilled, or manually. After review, the extra detail moved right under the chosen option and the text got shorter, so the choice and its effect read together.

3 · Edge cases

Designed, not left for later

Real orders rarely follow the happy path, so each tricky case got its own flow.

Capture earlyFor orders that take longer than the payment window.
Expired paymentA clear warning, and an email 2 days before.
Several shipmentsPayment is captured only after the last item ships.
Third-party warehousesCapture happens when the warehouse marks the order fulfilled.
4 · Scope

What we left out on purpose

Buyers see no new messages at checkout or in emails, so nothing about payment timing confuses them. And the manual fulfil page got no new message, because the engineering effort outweighed the benefit.
